# Teacher guide: run your first class in 10 minutes

For CIC instructors and partner-school teachers. You don't need to know how to code: the platform teaches,
you manage the class.

## 1. Sign in (1 minute)

- **CIC instructors:** sign in at `/login` with the account CIC gave you.
- **Teachers outside CIC:** go to `/signup`, choose **Teach a class**, and create a free account.

## 2. Create your class (2 minutes)

1. Go to **My classes → New class**.
2. **Name it** (e.g. "AI Academy · Period 2") and add your school.
3. **Add students** (optional). Upload the roster CSV exported from Google Classroom or your SIS, or paste one
   name per line. The preview shows who was found. Only first names and last initials are kept.
   Students who aren't on the roster can add themselves when they join.
4. **Tick the modules** you want to open. Students only ever see assigned modules, in order.
5. Click **Create class and get the join code**.

## 3. Get students in (1 minute)

Project the class page: it shows a big **join code** and a **QR code**. Students go to `/join` (or scan the QR),
type the code, and tap their name. No email, no password, no Google login. Works on Chromebooks and phones.

Each student gets a 6-character **resume code** on their journey page to continue on another device.
Print them all from **Print resume codes** for students without a fixed device.

## 4. Teach (the platform does the explaining)

Each module walks students through: **pretest → video → slides → quick check → worksheet → Python exercise →
exit ticket → posttest**. The next module unlocks when they finish. Students who join late get a 2-minute
**catch-up check** that skips modules they already know.

While they work, open your class page:

| You see | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Stuck** (red) | On the same step for 15+ minutes, 3+ failed attempts, or hand raised. Go there first. |
| **Not started** | Hasn't joined or done anything yet. |
| **Hands raised** | Student pressed *Raise hand*. Click **Helped** when done. |
| **Where the class is stuck** | Share of students with 2+ failed runs or hints on each concept. |
| **Reteach … tomorrow** | Click **Build a 5-minute warm-up** for a ready plan based on your class's actual mistakes. |
| **Recent AI hints** | Every hint the tutor gave, so you can check its quality. |

Turn on **Live class mode** to refresh the dashboard every 5 seconds during class.

## 5. Common tasks

- **A student got a new Chromebook:** in the student's row, open **⋯ → Let them sign in on a new device**. Their progress is kept.
- **Open the next module:** tick it under **Assigned modules**.
- **Grades for credit:** click **Gradebook CSV** (completion, pretest, posttest and gain per module).
- **Remove a student:** **⋯ → Remove from class** (deletes their progress).

## FAQ

**Can students see the answers?** No. Answer keys and solutions never leave the server, and pre/post tests
never reveal which answers were right.

**Does the AI just give students the code?** No. Hints are guiding questions. Any hint that contains a line
of the solution is thrown away and replaced with a safe one. You can read every hint on your dashboard.

**What if the internet is slow or drops?** Before class (on good Wi-Fi) have students press **Save for offline** on their journey page. Lessons and Python then work without internet, answers wait on the device and sync automatically when the connection returns. Students can also turn on *Lite mode* from the accessibility menu.

**Students keep asking how to do things?** Point them to the round **CIC Helper** button in the corner: it answers how-to questions in their language (but never does their exercises).
